League of Legends Tracker

Click any champion to cycle it through Locked → Unlocked → Banned → Locked. Your progress is saved automatically in this browser.

Rules

  1. Losing bans the champion. Lose a game and that champion is banned. You can’t play it again for the rest of the run.
  2. Unlock champions instead of picking them. You start with 5 random unlocked champions (a random roll, an ARAM reroll, or a spin of a wheel).
  3. Only good wins unlock a new champion. You unlock one new random champion after a win, but only if your KDA was positive, meaning (kills + assists) ÷ deaths is above 1. A win with a neutral or negative KDA unlocks nothing, though the champion you played stays available.
  4. The run ends when you have no unbanned champions left. If every champion you’ve unlocked is banned, you’ve failed.
